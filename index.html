From discovery to a useful shortlist

How to use a PikoBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet becomes useful when each row records the exact item you are considering. Keep the product option and unanswered questions beside the price so similar-looking finds are easier to compare.

1

Identify the exact item

Find the product on CAFANS and record its source URL, seller, color, size and quantity. Confirm that your intended shopping agent can handle that listing before you plan a purchase.

2

Check fit and evidence

Compare listing measurements with an item you own. Ask for missing dimensions, fabric details or photographs of the selected option. Keep seller statements separate from details you have checked yourself.

3

Review the full cost

Record the current item price and obtain relevant delivery and service quotes. Read the applicable return conditions and confirm the final payable amount before you commit.

Research notes

Keep your product list useful

Make each saved row traceable

Save a source link and a checked date alongside every product. Include the selected variant: a price for one size, color or quantity may not apply to another option. When details change, update the row before comparing it with a new find.

The CSV export on this page includes the original CAFANS product record, source price and snapshot date. Add seller replies, measurements and your own current checks when you open the file in your spreadsheet app.

Treat missing details as open questions

A short product title is a browsing label. It does not confirm fabric composition, fit, stock or agent eligibility. Record specific questions such as “confirm chest width in centimetres” instead of assuming that a familiar size label is enough.

If you receive warehouse photos, compare the exact item, size, color, quantity and visible condition against your order. Review the applicable inspection and return process with the service handling your purchase.

PikoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a PikoBuy spreadsheet?

A PikoBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, selected options, prices and shopping notes. This page combines a CAFANS catalog selection with comparison tools and guidance.

Do the buttons open PikoBuy?

No. The PikoBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons both open CAFANS. Confirm agent availability and the exact source listing at the destination before ordering.

Are the displayed prices the final delivered cost?

No. Prices are estimated USD amounts captured from CAFANS on November 19, 2026. Shipping, fees, taxes and exchange-rate changes may affect the amount you pay.

Are the catalog images verified QC photographs?

The images are catalog images sourced from CAFANS. They are not presented as verified warehouse QC photographs or proof of authenticity.

How do I save and export products?

Select Save pick on the cards you want to keep, then choose Export saved picks. Your CSV contains product names, categories, estimated prices, original source URLs and the source-check date. Picks stay in memory for this page session; refreshing clears the selection.

Is this selection updated automatically?

No. This is a six-product snapshot checked on 19 November 2026. Open CAFANS to review its current catalog and confirm the details of any item you are considering.
